The pile that split in two

A second mistake of mine turned up today, this time not from playing but from the document debt list: dropping borrowed the wrong limit from the wrong routine and split a large pile into two where the original makes one.

Two routines, one limit, in the wrong place

GiveObj has its own, correct limit of 144 for merging into a pile: a full pile stops there and the rest spills into a new object. CmdDrop's own ground branch (objcmd.c:886-900) is a different routine, one unconditional AddObj call with no limit at all. PlaceOnGround borrowed GiveObj's limit into this other routine anyway, so a pile over 144 split into two separate piles on the ground.

Reachable with the data already shipped

The world already carries piles up to 240, built without ever going through a merge, so a player could walk onto such a pile, pick it straight up and drop it right back down without the limit ever firing along the way. The fix is one call for the whole count, exactly what the original does.

A side effect: a second stale entry

The document debt list had recorded the gap, by name and by line, months ago. This time the entry was current rather than stale, and closing it was a one-routine change.
