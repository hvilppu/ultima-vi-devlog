Points Never Pause the Player

The leader's and the party's own movement-point charge is in the engine now. It was the last piece the previous measuring pass found, and on the way there was one more question measuring could not answer: what to do about the one place the real game would actually stop the player.

The original does not ask permission to move, it asks permission to give control back

RunNPCTurns runs after every player command until the actor it picks is AI_COMMAND, the player's own turn. If the leader's points get zeroed because a follower ran dry (last post's §2.21), the loop never finds the player eligible, and it keeps running other actors and granting points until the leader qualifies again. The player is not stuck on a key press; the game just processes something else for a moment before handing control back.

In this engine one key press is one step and a fixed clock advance, not the minute clock an earlier architecture choice deliberately left unbuilt. So the question was real: should the player's own movement stop when the party's points run out, or are the points just bookkeeping? Asked, and the answer was the latter: the points are real and measurable, but a key press always works exactly as it does now.

Where the points actually go

The leader pays SubTerrainMov from the square it is leaving before moving, exactly as a character walking an errand does, and exactly as the previous post measured. Every member whose behaviour is Follow pays a flat ten points, whether or not it actually moved or was already in formation. The Avatar is object number 1, outside the governed range of 2 to 223, so it needs its own hourly grant; the rest of the party (2, 3, 4) already sit inside that range and get their DEXTE from the same grant as every other governed character.

Diagonal movement is not costed separately (PartyAdvance's own +5), because this view has no diagonal input at all: the keyboard picks one of four directions, never two at once. Vehicles (ship, skiff, raft, balloon) and the mounted horse halving are still waiting their turn, since none of them are built.

The follower charge moved out so it could be tested

The charge was not left inside the view, even though that is where it was first written: it is now U6PartyFollow.SpendFollowerPoints, called after the formation step rather than inside it. Same split as everywhere else in this project: the rule belongs to the engine, the view just calls it. Three new tests pin the rule down: ten for every follower, never for the leader, and nothing at all for anyone not in Follow.
