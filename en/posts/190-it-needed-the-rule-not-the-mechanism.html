It needed the rule not the mechanism

One piece of the visibility module was left: a wall the party cannot see from its own side does not show what stands on it. I wrote in two documents that it needs an object stack, which this project's composer does not keep as a list. It did not. It is built now, and the module is seven slices for seven.

A mechanism is not a rule

The original does this by unlinking the square's whole display stack and returning its nodes to the free list. I read that, saw the stack, and wrote down that a stack is what this needs.

But the stack is the mechanism. The rule is "nothing on this square is drawn", and that is a per square test. This composer already has one choke point every object and character tile passes through, because the hidden and dark refusals live there. The gate went in beside them, as a third refusal.

That is the same mistake read two ways: I described a building block as a requirement, and I wrote it down in a form the next reader would have believed. Both documents now say so.

East and south, and the pair that changes nothing

The gate tests two neighbours: east and south. If either is invisible, what stands on the wall is hidden. The reason is that the original's walls are drawn from the north west: a wall seen only from behind would otherwise show the torches and signs hanging on its far face.

The test drives each of them alone, and also the two that are not tested: west and north change nothing. That second pair is what makes the first a measurement of those two neighbours rather than of hiding in general.

And worth noting: this condition is written correctly for two different neighbours, where the light source pass one routine later writes its own condition twice as identical copies. One file, two loops, one careful and one not.

One number that has moved four times

One test counts how many (square, centre) pairs have an object on a lit square. Its value has moved three times during this arc, and every move is a measurement: 

35073   before the visibility fill, with a square lit area
21429   with the fill and no light sources
27153   after the light source pass
25480   after the wall gate

The numbers are in the test's own comment with their causes, because a bare value would only say that something changed. And the same test's first claim, that no dark or hidden square carries an object, stays at nought throughout: that is the half which keeps the drops honest.
