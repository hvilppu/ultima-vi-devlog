One hit point and karma worth of experience

Resurrection is built. Two of the world interface's 33 members still refuse, and one of those answers as soon as the conversation scripts are handed over, which in play is always. So one is really left: the player's own item selection.

What reviving does, in order

the body's quality names the character
the character is moved to where the body (or its carrier) is
the body's contents come back, the amulet worn rather than packed
the body is deleted
ClrDead
hit points = 1
shape = the original shape
magic = full
ClrAilmentsProtected   (dead, poisoned, asleep, paralysed, protected)
experience = experience * karma / 100   (only when karma < 98)
placement: growing square rings, radius 1 to 4
a party member rejoins

The dead bit is cleared twice, because ClrDead comes first and ClrAilmentsProtected clears it again among five bits. Reproduced as it stands: the order of writes to one byte is what a save file shows.

The karma bound is a measurement, not a rounding

Experience is cut only when karma is below 98. Karma 98 keeps every point. And the division truncates towards nought: 99 points at karma 50 is 49, not 50.

The multiply is 32 bit in the original, which is a rule too: 60000 points at karma 97 is 58200, and it does not fit in 16 bits on the way.

Placement: the corners are tried twice

The character is placed in growing square rings around the body, and each step tries four squares: the top edge left to right, the left edge top to bottom, the bottom edge right to left, and the right edge bottom to top.

At step nought the first two candidates are the same top left corner, and the last two the same bottom right. At the last step the pairs coincide the other way round. So a ring of 8r squares costs 8r+4 tests. The duplicates show up nowhere except in how many times the square test is asked, which is why they are left in place: a tidier version would ask a different number of questions.

Two new anomalies

The opcode is narrower than the spell. The conversation opcode looks for exactly one corpse type (0x153), while the resurrect spell accepts four: the dead body, the mouse, the dead gargoyle and the dead cyclops. Both run the same revive routine, so a script cannot resurrect a gargoyle and the spell can.

And the opcode skips both of the spell's gates: quality >= 0xE0 (a temporary monster) and quality 0 ("the corpse is too old"). At quality 0 the character number read is nought, which is not a character but the vehicle slot.

And with no body the original writes at index -1. The loop ends on -1 when the chain holds no corpse, and the revive runs anyway: five fields written in front of the array. This is the fourth finding of that shape in this project. It is not reproduced: a write outside an array is not a rule, and the difference is named in the code.
