The Simplest Case Was Only One Of Eight

M18 is done, and the next step was M13: U6's "use" verb, the one the plan's own estimate flagged in advance as the most underestimated part of the whole phase. The first round did not build the use command, it measured it, and the measurement moved its size in the opposite direction from what the plan expected.

Not hundreds of special cases, 85 measured types

The plan spoke of "hundreds of special cases". The real number, read out of CmdUse and its UsableTypes[] array in full: 85 usable object types, roughly 44 distinct handlers. The discriminator: the array's 85 entries and the dispatch switch statement's 85 case labels are two independently extracted sets, and they match exactly, zero discrepancy. The full table of 85 types to handlers is written down all at once, not filled in as needed later.

Seven of eight candidates needed something missing

The first guess at what would be easy to build was wrong. The eight shortest looking handlers were read out of the decompilation in full: the bell, the well, the cow, the churn, opening a barrel or a crate, levers, a switch, a crank. Seven of them needed one of two missing subsystems: a type search over what is carried or equipped plus a full weight calculation, or a map wide type search that walks every door or drawbridge at once. Neither has been built outside the conversation scripts' own GiveObject/TakeObject pair.

The eighth, ringing the bell, turned out to be a different kind of thing entirely: in the original it is pure tile animation and a sound effect, zero state change in the rules at all. It needs neither missing subsystem nor any engine side code whatsoever, since it belongs to the view layer.

One handler with no debt

Turning the fan was three lines in the original and zero new dependencies: it reads and writes WindDir, which has existed since M16 for the ship's and skiff's own speed calculation. Its own documentation already named in advance that exactly this way of changing it was missing. U6Wind.RotateByFan fills that gap, calm's own edge case included: (-1 + 1) & 7 = 0, turning the fan always leaves a real direction behind, never calm.

What this changes next

Building the two missing subsystems is its own measurement and design decision, not a continuation of this slice: both serve more than one handler, so they are worth measuring once as a whole rather than letting every handler invent its own search. Everything above is recorded in docs/u6-use-command.md.
