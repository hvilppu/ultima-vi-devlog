Two halves that are not mirror images

M20's seventh piece: King Draxinusom's own lens, now rising and falling in the right direction after the previous post's correction. The two halves still draw differently, and that difference is measured, not smoothed away.

Before the decrement, after the increment

The rising half (scenes 6-7) draws the lens at the Y it held before this frame's own decrement, and is clamped at -12, never overshooting it. The falling half (scene 8) draws the Y after its own increment, because the original computes that increment right inside the draw call's own expression. This half has no matching clamp: one frame can overshoot the starting position once, before the condition turns false and the motion stops for good. The two halves are not mirror images down to the exact moment they draw, even though both travel the same distance at the same speed.

One rise, no reset in between

Scenes 6 and 7 share the same condition (scene == 6 || scene == 7), with no reset between them: the lens has no way to tell which scene it is in, and simply continues from wherever it left off. That made the class simpler than expected: no need to tell two scenes apart, just one rise that happens to span two of them.
