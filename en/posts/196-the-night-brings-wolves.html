The night brings wolves

Camping has been built for a long time: the fire, the meal, the guard, the party falling unconscious, waking, and the three conditions that gate healing. One thing was missing, with its reason written down: the night monster encounter needs EGG_generate, which means a monster and combat system. M14 landed, so the reason was stale. The same generator the summon spell already drives makes the wolves.

The gate is three tests, and the order is the rule

A rest runs in ten minute steps. On each step: 

1. !(i & 5)                    takes no draw at all
2. OSI_rand(1, 64) == 1        one draw
3. HasNightEncounter()         the encounter's own draws

&& short circuits, and every draw comes from the one shared stream, so the order is not a detail: it decides where in that stream every later draw in the game sits.

And the first test is strange

!(i & 5) is true only when i mod 8 is 0 or 2. So two of every eight steps are looked at. The decompilation's own comment calls the condition strange, and with reason: & 5 is bits 0 and 2, which matches no natural period in ten minute steps.

A nine hour rest is 54 steps, 14 of them checked. Two plausible wrong readings, each pinned by its own test: every step would make the night four times as dangerous, and every hour would look right without being right.

The four sides are not symmetric

side     x               y
left     MapX - 6        MapY + incr - 6
top      MapX + incr-6   MapY - 7
right    MapX + 7        MapY + incr - 6
bottom   MapX + incr-6   MapY + 6

The view is eleven squares wide, so squares -5..+5. Left and bottom are therefore one square outside it, right and top two. And the strip along a side is not centred: the offset is 0..10 with six taken off, so it runs -6..+4.

A symmetric reading is the plausible wrong one here, and it would have been easy to write by accident: one constant instead of four, and the code would look tidier.

Draw accounting, and what a full creature table costs

type                          1 draw   (one for the whole group)
count 1-8                     1 draw
per try: side + offset        2 draws  (up to 8 tries)

One monster that lands on its first try costs four draws. And here is the part that must not be tidied: the original never tests EGG_generate's answer, so with the monster slots full all eight tries are still drawn for a creature that does not exist. A tidy implementation would skip the loop and leave the stream somewhere else.

Two wake reasons that were unreachable until now

The original has three ways for the night to end, and all three are reachable now:

  • Nothing happened. Healing, if the three conditions hold.
  • The guard heard something. The line prints and the party wakes.
  • No guard. The party stays asleep, and nothing is printed.

The third is the one a player wants to avoid: with nobody on watch, the encounter wakes no one.

One named gap

The guard's own turn (NPC_TakeTurn) is not wired: the night loop runs no NPC turns at all, so a posted guard stands still through the night. The encounter does not depend on the guard moving, so this is a named gap rather than a broken rule.
