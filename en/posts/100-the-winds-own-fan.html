The wind's own fan

An earlier slice refused the four wind spells, calling the wind's own path unmeasured. It turned out to be measured already, in three separate pieces nobody had written together yet: the spread table and its pair accessor, the missile walk and its blocking rule, and the wind's own handler. This slice is assembly, not measurement.

Five destinations around the target, not the caster

MagicWind builds five destinations around the selected square: the square itself, one step either side along the spread table's own pair, and a second step along those same two directions. Only the walk itself starts at the caster; the destinations never do. The new U6SpellWindPath.Build assembles the five destinations and calls the existing missile line of fire five times, once per ray, without restating its own blocking rule.

The interleaving the brief itself called untraced

The brief said plainly: the original walks all five projectiles together, one step each per tick, and whether an actor caught by two rays would appear once or twice in the result had not been traced. Reading the original's own stepping routine further settled both questions: one shared bitmap collects every one of the five projectiles' hits, and the final list is only built from it after the walk ends, in ascending object number order. The same actor can never appear twice, and interleaving cannot change the order. This slice reproduces that by taking the union of five independent ray results rather than concatenating them ray by ray.

Finding: wiring it in would need a routine that has never been built

Feeding the fan to the wind's own handler was not enough to make the spells actually run. The handler also demands its own case's victim effect input, and three of the four winds would need a "deal damage to an actor" routine on top of that, which this project has never built for any spell. Inventing a stand-in for it would be exactly the mistake that left the path itself unmeasured for so long, so the view's own wiring was left untouched, and the finding was written down in its place instead.
