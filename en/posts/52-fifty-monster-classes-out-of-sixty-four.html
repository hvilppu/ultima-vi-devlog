Fifty Monster Classes Out Of Sixty Four

M14, combat and magic, opened this round. Its first slice, the player's Attack command and combat.c's own tables, is measured and built. Both in the same session, which is unusual: the measuring stopped at the usual handover point, but the owner asked for it to carry straight on into building, and that call is theirs to make.

Ten bytes that would have looked right

combat.c's data sits ten bytes lower in the owner's own GAME.EXE than the decompilation's own address comments claim, while spell.c's comments are exact against the same base. A wrong reading would not have crashed anything: it would have handed back an armour table starting at six instead of one, a perfectly plausible run of type numbers. That is why every table in this slice is found by searching the whole 205,613 byte program for its own shape, four independent searches, each hitting exactly one place, none of them computed from a decompilation address.

Fifty classes over one bit

Granting experience turned out to depend on one bit, not on the whole alignment value. u6.h names four values with two bits, and "attacks the player" is only the lower one. Seventeen of the sixty four monster classes are EVIL and thirty three are CHAOTIC, and both carry that bit, so a gate written as "alignment is CHAOTIC" would have granted experience to the thirty three and silently denied it to the seventeen. The engine had no value of its own for this at all. The same defect shape as NameOf and IsInParty before it: a rule read from the surrounding code rather than from the handler itself, which looked sensible right up until the bits were counted separately.

What the slice does not cover

The turn order collides with the owner's earlier decision that play time keeps no minute clock, so it needs its own measuring session and its own decision before anything is built. Spells are entirely unmeasured, spell.c is 2,802 lines of code rather than a table. The missile flight's blocking rules and spread tiles were read only far enough to know they are unmeasured. The alarm and vigilante path reaches into karma and guard modes this slice does not build, and was read once rather than twice.
