The rules are done, the wiring is not

M20's ninth and tenth piece in one post: the three dissolve triggers and the scene advance. With them, every measured rule in the scene state machine is its own tested class. None of them is wired to anything yet.

Three triggers, three different step list lengths

The Codex appears and opens with two draw-then-dissolve steps each; the lenses' light with one. A single combined shape could have forced an invented second step onto the middle one, but that would have been made up rather than measured. The class keeps the list exactly as long as the original actually is.

Scenes 6 and 7 are not special for their page

The scene advance's own switch looks like it singles out scenes 6 and 7, but both show the same page the ordinary rule would have shown anyway. They are special only for the music and the lens reset. Scenes 14 and 15 are the opposite case: they do not change the page, they drop it entirely, because their own picture comes from somewhere else.

What is not here

Running the dissolve itself, a real screen, a timer, and whatever in the game actually calls all of this are not here. Every class is a rule, not a view. M20's own "done when" (the ending plays through) is not met by this, only brought closer to it.
